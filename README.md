## 🌟 Overview

Traditional makeup tutorials rely on 2D video feeds that require constant pausing, guessing opposite-side angles, and squinting away from your mirror.

**UR Artist** bridges haute couture beauty with computational vision. Powered by Apple TrueDepth 3D sensors, CoreML, and the Apple Neural Engine, the **UR Artist Intelligent Mirror** dynamically calculates your unique facial geometry to project millimeter-accurate application zones onto your live reflection.

### Technical & Architectural Highlights
- **0.1mm Real-Time Landmark Tracking**: Sub-millimeter tracking across orbital rims, nasal bridges, zygomatic contours, and lip vermilion borders.
- **Bilateral Opposite-Side Symmetry**: Automatically computes inverse geometric vectors so eyeliner wings and cheek contouring match symmetrically.
- **100% On-Device Privacy Architecture**: All sensor streams and biometric landmark calculations run strictly on the Apple Neural Engine. Zero video frames or biometric telemetry ever leave the device.
